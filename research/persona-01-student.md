# Persona interview 01 — Maya, 20, university student (bio, pre-med)

Context: part-time coffee shop job, aging iPhone, iOS Calendar with ~6 events all
from other people's invites. Real system = camera roll + a Notes list rewritten
every Sunday.

## 1. The incident
`BIOL2041_handbook_v3_FINAL.pdf`, 22 pages. Assessment dates in a table on p.14,
written as **"Wk 7 (Fri)"** — not real dates. Resolving them needs a separate
term-week table on p.3. Screenshotted it on the bus at 11% battery, never went
back. Missed a 1,500-word critical appraisal, submitted 19h late, capped at 40%.

> "The date wasn't a date. It was 'Wk 7 (Fri)'."

## 2. What she does today
Camera roll IS the database (~4,000 images). Has screenshotted the same timetable
five times because she couldn't find the earlier one. Calendar apps don't stick:
~8 taps per event, always entered while walking or half-asleep. An empty calendar
feels bad, so she stops opening it, so it stays empty. Abandoned 6 productivity
apps; deleted one *during* its onboarding quiz.

## 3. Recurrence
Labs alternate weeks but the pattern FLIPS after reading week (A,B,A,B,gap,B,A).
Tutorials skip reading week, lectures don't. Coffee shop rota drops Wednesday for
the following week; shifts swap by text constantly.

> "A blank calendar is neutral. A lying calendar is actively poisonous."
> "I'd genuinely rather you put in eight separate one-off events than guess
> 'every Tuesday' and be wrong twice."

## 4. Deadlines vs events
Hard = coursework 23:59 cutoffs, exams, shifts. Soft = society talks, revision
sessions. **Labels are decoration; behaviour is real.** Hard = nags the night
before and again in the morning. Soft = never buzzes. If the distinction is only
a colour or tag, she won't look at it.

## 5. Sharing
Only realistic case: group project, 4 people, she read the brief.
**Her friends will NOT tap "add 14 events."** Reads like handing over your keys.
What they WOULD accept: a clean **image** of the list, pasteable into the group
chat, that they screenshot into their own camera roll.

> "Your killer sharing feature is the one you probably think is the boring fallback."

## 6. After a bad import (30 events, 6 wrong)
Notices ~2, fixes 1, gets bored, leaves the rest. A week later a wrong alert fires
and she wants it all gone. Does NOT want bulk correction ("that's a spreadsheet
and I won't do a spreadsheet on a phone"). Wants **one button: delete everything
from this import.** One-tap batch undo is what makes a second attempt thinkable.

> "If undo means hunting 30 events out of my calendar one by one, I delete the app
> AND the app has vandalised my calendar."

## 7. Dealbreakers
- Any account creation before she sees it work = out. Let her shoot the poster first.
- Google account request in session 1 = hard no (uni email is Google = her degree).
- "Allow access to all photos" = no. Use iOS limited selection, or camera-first.
- Will not pay a subscription. One-off GBP 3-5 maybe. GBP 4.99/mo is absurd to her.
- **3 taps** poster-to-done. 4 if the confirm screen shows what it found. A
  "review 12 extracted items" screen while standing in a corridor = closes it forever.

## 8. Verdict
Installs it, because camera-roll-as-database is genuinely her life. Dies in the
middle: fine on a one-date poster, then fails or half-succeeds on the 22-page
handbook and the photo-of-a-printed-rota-with-handwritten-swaps.

> "The moment your app becomes a source of information rather than a photo of one,
> it has to be right."
> "Build the one-tap undo before you build the smart recurrence. And don't make me
> sign in."
